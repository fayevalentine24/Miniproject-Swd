About Me

I'm a graphic designer specializing in brutalist aesthetics, bold poster typography, album artwork, and streetwear graphics. My work centers on high-impact layouts, raw textures, and distinct visual hierarchies that bridge subculture style with sharp visual storytelling—whether I'm crafting drop-shoulder apparel designs, editorial poster art, or album covers.
